THE CALCULATION

Five governed stages, two exact explanations

A coefficient such as 0.80 is an action weight, not 0.80 wins. The weights create signed evidence inside one game; the final number is the player’s share of that team-game result.

  1. 1
    Reprice frozen facts

    Apply the selected play-level coefficients to immutable semantic events.

  2. 2
    Classify components

    Place every signed component in Offense, Defense, or Other.

  3. 3
    Measure six contexts

    Calculate General, Teammate, and Opponent factors for both sides and split their joint effect with Shapley values.

  4. 4
    Adjust and close

    Apply context without flipping signs, floor negative final shares, and close each team exactly to one.

  5. 5
    Allocate responsibility

    Scale component evidence with its side, then run the exact responsibility allocator.

SIX-FACTOR CONTEXT

Context explains how the lineup changed raw value

The expanded view shows General O, Teammate O, Opponent O, General D, Teammate D, and Opponent D. The display names the side affected: Opponent O is derived from opponent defense, while Opponent D is derived from opponent offense. The collapsed view is the exact sum of those same columns—never a second calculation.

BROWSER-LOCAL EXPERIMENTS

Change the formula without sending results anywhere

On desktop, the Experiment Lab presents ten clearer play-credit sliders—positive scoring, playmaking, offensive rebounding, missed shots, turnovers, offensive shortfalls, defensive rebounding, helpers/hustle, defense, and fouls/administration—plus all 68 Advanced coefficients, six context magnifiers, and offense/defense K and lambda. Each play-credit slider runs from 0× to 2× with Original centered at 1×. The ten controls are translated deterministically into the frozen six-group configuration plus exact coefficient overrides. When one category is amplified, policy remainder decreases first; if necessary, the other unedited roles in that complete play are reduced proportionally to keep the allocation at one possession. Explicit Advanced edits are protected and never changed automatically. If protected values alone cannot fit, the run remains blocked with an error.

Season packages are verified before calculation. Configurations, checkpoints, and results stay in IndexedDB on this device. There are no experiment-result network writes. Ranking cards are rendered as PNG images in the browser; nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly share the finished image through your device.

INTERPRETATION AND LIMITS

It measures this recorded system, not every basketball skill

Value Contributed depends on the accuracy and coverage of recorded play-by-play, lineups, tracking inputs, and governed role inference. It does not make unrecorded communication, scheme, or off-ball influence directly observable. Context is an explicit model contribution—not proof of individual causation.

Use browser experiments to inspect sensitivity to defensive context and small off-court samples. Those what-if calculations stay local and do not replace the immutable public Original baseline.

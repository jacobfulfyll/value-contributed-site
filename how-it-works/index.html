How Value Contributed works

The stat reads each game possession by possession, gives players credit or blame for recorded actions, adjusts that raw value for exact possession context, preserves whether each result is positive or negative, and closes every team-game to one signed-gross share.

THE BIG PICTURE

One game becomes a set of player shares

Value Contributed does not say that a block is literally worth 0.8 wins. A number such as 0.80 is an action weight applied before the game is normalized. The final result answers: what share of this team’s game value belongs to each player?

  1. 1
    Read the possession

    Identify the shot, pass, rebound, turnover, defensive play, foul, and any linked contributors recorded in the data.

  2. 2
    Price each action

    Combine points or possession value with the action weights shown below. Credits are positive; mistakes are negative.

  3. 3
    Apply exact possession context

    Adjust each nonzero action using leave-target-out teammate performance, opponent strength, rest, and exact exposure.

  4. 4
    Build both time modes

    Recompute all_minutes and competitive from their own retained possessions.

  5. 5
    Keep the signed player totals

    Positive contributions remain positive, penalties remain negative, and zero-value or DNP players remain zero.

  6. 6
    Close the team with signed gross

    Divide by total absolute player value and assign the exact closing residual only across positive players.

This public dashboard contains only the completed V8 responsibility result. Value Contributed sums V8 across every selected game; Wins Contributed sums it only in games the player’s team won.

V8 FOUR-FACTOR CONTEXT + RESPONSIBILITY

V8 separates how value changes from who owns each side

V8 begins with the frozen V7 action ledger, then applies four exact player-game context factors: teammate offense, teammate defense, opponent offense, and opponent defense. The teammate factors compare player-on and player-off team performance at the exact possession lineups. The opponent factors use target-game-excluded lineup models, so the game being explained cannot train its own opponent-strength adjustment.

Context composition Raw VC + teammate O + opponent O + teammate D + opponent D = Final VC
Official side responsibility Offensive VC + Defensive VC + Other VC ≈ Final VC

Those are two different views of the same final amount. The dashboard evaluates all 16 combinations of the four persisted context factors and uses an order-independent Shapley split to express each factor's effect in additive Final VC units. Context composition therefore explains how the player’s value moved after the official team close. Responsibility allocates that already-final value to nonnegative offensive, defensive, and genuinely side-neutral Other amounts using stored positive and negative evidence. It never changes Final VC, rank, or any context factor. Hustle is not a V8 final side: screen, rebound, and boxout activity is routed to offense or defense. Both displayed decompositions close to Final VC within audited binary64 tolerance; machine-precision residuals are not treated as substantive context effects.

The rankings page sums the immutable per-game allocations. Its Value Contributed view includes all selected games; its Wins Contributed view applies the same stored amounts only to wins. Each of the five context percentages is its summed additive amount divided by the same selected Final VC or WC total. A zero total displays as not applicable.

V6 POSSESSION CONTEXT + SIGNED-GROSS CLOSE

Actions keep their sign, then every team still closes to one

V6 prices causal possession outcomes first and applies teammate and opponent context to each nonzero action. Context may change magnitude, but its sign floor prevents credit from becoming a penalty or a penalty from becoming credit. No seconds multiplier is applied afterward because action volume and exact possession exposure already represent playing time.

Player-game total sum(final context-adjusted action values)
Signed-gross denominator G = sum(abs(player-game total))
Base share player-game total / G
Positive residual close the amount needed to reach 1.0, split across positive players in proportion to positive total

Negative players retain their negative gross share; positive players receive the closing residual; zero players stay zero. Each team’s player shares sum to exactly 1.0. The competitive mode excludes only the final sustained Q4/OT garbage window: if a comeback crosses back below the threshold, the prior minutes become competitive again.

EARLIER V5 COMPARISON

How impressive was the result against who actually played?

Player ORtg and DRtg come from the full-game rating input. A nonpositive rating falls back, in order, to its positive factor reconstruction, the player’s season rating, and the team’s game rating. Each opponent’s expected strength reflects the players and minutes in that game.

Offensive performance (actual team ORtg − opponent lineup DRtg) / opponent lineup DRtg
Defensive performance (opponent lineup ORtg − opponent actual ORtg) / opponent lineup ORtg
Rating exposure player possessions / team possessions = 5 × selected player seconds / selected team-player seconds
Offensive rating quality player ORtg / teammate ORtg
Defensive rating quality teammate DRtg / player DRtg (lower player DRtg is better)
Teammate-rating multiplier rating quality ^ (rating exposure × rating impact)
After teammate ratings positive complete side value × rating multiplier, normalized to the same team side budget
Opponent side factors offense: exp(2 × offense performance); defense: exp(2 × defense performance)
Fixed-budget side targets post-rating side value × opponent factor, normalized back to the same offense + defense budget
Player opponent adjustment fixed side transfer × (player post-rating side value × exposure) / team sum
Final V5 share opponent-adjusted offense + defense + other, closed nonnegative to the unchanged team total

The current V5 run uses teammate rating impact 1 and no multiplier bound. Rating impact is the teammate-quality lever: 0 makes ratings neutral, values below 1 damp them, and values above 1 enhance them. Competitive exposure uses non-garbage-time seconds; all-minutes exposure uses the full game. Because exposure is a share of team possessions, team pace cancels out. Raw actions still receive sec_fact^0.5 first, so recorded production and playing time remain the value seed. Both the rating and opponent stages preserve exactly one team total and floor negative player totals before proportional closure. Opponent context has its own impact lever, currently 2. It exponentially determines the complete fixed offense-versus-defense transfer; exposure then gives more of that credit or debit to players with more post-rating side value and more time on the floor. It changes which side drove the game without creating additional team value. Defended made and missed threes both use a 0.35 responsibility rate. Screen assists, secondary assists, offensive box outs, and jump creation feed the offensive side magnitude; defensive box outs feed defense.

MADE-SHOT CREDIT

The type of basket changes who gets credit

The current policy starts with the shot’s full face value—two or three points—then applies these shares to the recorded shooter and assister. A self-created jumper therefore gives substantially more credit to the shooter than an alley-oop does.

Shot type Shooter share Assister share

These are actor shares, not final VC. Rebounds, screens, secondary assists, and other linked actions can redistribute parts of the same possession’s value before team-game normalization.

SHOT DEFENSE

Defended shots and blocks use shot location

A defended miss earns shot points × location rate. A defended make produces a penalty of −(shot points − opponent possession value) × rate. An exact block uses the shot’s face value and a rate ten percentage points above the defended-shot rate.

CURRENT LIMITS

What this calculation still does not see

No gravity or deterrence estimate

It does not yet measure defenders avoiding a rim protector or defenders staying attached to an off-ball shooter.

No loose-ball or deflection bonus

Those counts are excluded because their overlap with steals, rebounds, and possession changes is not reliably resolved.

Team rebounds are not player rebounds

A team rebound remains audit information and is never spread across individual players.

Calculated, not formally certified

The complete local calculation passed its current audits, but source freeze and independent certification remain separate.

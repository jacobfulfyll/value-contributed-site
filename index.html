How to read the table Wins VC is value in wins; VC/game sorts require 20 games.

VC is the player’s final team-value share across selected games; Wins VC includes wins only and Loss VC includes losses. Offense, Defense, Hustle, and Other add back to total VC.

Post VC/G Δ compares postseason and regular-season VC per game; Post rank Δ compares Wins VC rank. Positive means improvement. Postseason combines Play-In and playoff games.

The eight audit columns show raw offense/defense and each calculation-stage change. Percentages use gross raw value; the stages compound and are not independent estimates.
